# 1. Purpose

This document serves as the enterprise knowledge base for stitching, tailoring, garment assembly, construction techniques, finishing methods, and manufacturing terminology used by FCOS AI Studio Enterprise.

It provides standardized stitching terminology, apparel manufacturing concepts, tailoring practices, garment finishing techniques, and fashion industry knowledge used by the FCOS Fashion Intelligence Engine.
