# Description Engine Specification

Version: FCOS AI Studio Enterprise v2.1.1

Status: Architecture Frozen

---

# Purpose

The Description Engine generates premium, SEO-optimized, luxury editorial product descriptions for every catalog item processed by FCOS Enterprise Studio.

Its objective is to produce descriptions that read as though they were independently authored by an experienced luxury fashion copywriter while remaining completely faithful to the available product evidence.

This specification governs only Individual Description generation.

It does not define Product Naming, Style Information, Measurement Rules, or Validation Rules.

---

# Description Objectives

Every Individual Description shall be:

- Unique for every product
- Human readable
- Luxury editorial
- SEO optimized
- Factually accurate
- Evidence driven
- Commercially persuasive
- Natural sounding
- Free from repetitive wording
- Suitable for premium ecommerce platforms

---

# HTML Requirements

Only the following HTML tags are permitted:

<p>

<strong>

No other HTML tags shall be generated.

---

# Description Structure

Every description shall contain exactly FOUR paragraphs.

Paragraph 1

Luxury Editorial Opening

Paragraph 2

Fabric, Craftsmanship & Styling

Paragraph 3

Comfort & Construction

Paragraph 4

Style Information, Care Instruction & Fit & Delivery Information

No additional paragraphs are permitted.

---

# Paragraph 1

Length

50–70 words

Purpose

Introduce the product using luxury editorial storytelling.

Paragraph 1 shall include:

- Product Name (exactly once)
- Overall visual appeal
- Craftsmanship
- Premium aesthetic
- Silhouette
- Design language
- Editorial tone

The Product Name shall appear only once at the beginning of Paragraph 1.

It shall never appear again in the remaining description.

Example

<p><strong>[PRODUCT NAME]</strong> ...</p>

---

# Paragraph 2

Length

60–80 words

Purpose

Describe the garment experience.

Include:

- Fabric feel
- Fabric movement
- Print
- Embroidery
- Weaving
- Surface texture
- Decorative craftsmanship
- Exactly TWO approved occasions
- Exactly ONE styling suggestion

Only two occasions are permitted.

Only one styling suggestion is permitted.

# Narrative Intelligence Engine

The Narrative Intelligence Engine is responsible for ensuring that every Individual Description reads as though it has been independently authored by a professional luxury fashion copywriter rather than generated from a fixed template.

Narrative Intelligence shall automatically vary storytelling, vocabulary, sentence structure, pacing, and editorial tone while preserving factual accuracy and the required HTML structure.

Narrative selection shall be based on the combined analysis of:

- Product Type
- Product Subtype
- Construction
- Silhouette
- Style Type
- Design Aesthetic
- Occasion
- Fabric
- Work Detail
- Pattern Detail

The selected narrative shall remain fully supported by the Evidence Hierarchy.

# Narrative Rotation

The same narrative style shall not be repeatedly applied across consecutive catalog items unless the products belong to the same commercial collection.

FCOS shall automatically rotate vocabulary, sentence rhythm, paragraph openings, and storytelling approach to maximise uniqueness.

Narrative variation shall include:

- Different opening styles
- Different sentence lengths
- Different transitions
- Different descriptive vocabulary
- Different storytelling flow
- Different closing statements

while maintaining the required paragraph structure.
