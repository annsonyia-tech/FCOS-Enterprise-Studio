# FCOS Enterprise Studio
# Description Engine
Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Individual Description Engine used by FCOS Enterprise Studio.

Its responsibility is to generate unique, luxury editorial HTML descriptions for every product.

No product naming, style information extraction, or evidence hierarchy rules are defined in this document.

---

# Description Objectives

Every Individual Description shall be:

- 100% unique
- Luxury editorial in tone
- SEO optimized
- Human readable
- Brand consistent
- Free from keyword stuffing
- HTML formatted
- Factually accurate based on available evidence

---

# HTML Structure

Every description must contain exactly FOUR HTML paragraphs.

Paragraph 1

Luxury editorial introduction.

Paragraph 2

Fabric, craftsmanship, occasions and styling.

Paragraph 3

Comfort, finishing and construction quality.

Paragraph 4

Style Information, Care Instruction and Fit & Delivery Information.

No additional paragraphs are permitted.

---

# Paragraph Structure

## Paragraph 1

Length:

50–70 words

Content:

- Product Name in bold
- Overall visual appeal
- Craftsmanship
- Silhouette
- Premium aesthetic
- Editorial luxury tone

Format

<p><strong>[PRODUCT NAME]</strong> ...</p>

---

## Paragraph 2

Length:

60–80 words

Content:

- Fabric feel
- Fabric movement
- Print / Embroidery / Work details
- Exactly TWO approved occasions
- ONE styling suggestion

Requirements

Exactly two occasions only.

Examples

Wedding

Reception

Festive Celebration

Cocktail Party

Engagement Ceremony

Haldi

Mehendi

Temple Visit

Office Wear

Casual Wear

Vacation

Brunch

---

## Paragraph 3

Length:

30–50 words

Content

- Comfort
- Wearability
- Construction quality
- Finishing quality
- Ease of movement
- Durability

---

## Paragraph 4

Always use this format

<p>
<strong>Style Information:</strong>
[STYLE INFORMATION]
<br>

<strong>Care Instruction:</strong>
[CARE TEXT]
<br>

<strong>Fit & Delivery Information:</strong>
[DELIVERY TEXT]
</p>

No other content belongs in Paragraph 4.

# SEO Writing Rules

Every description shall:

- Begin with the Product Name.
- Naturally include the primary fabric.
- Naturally include the primary work or print.
- Mention the silhouette where relevant.
- Avoid keyword repetition.
- Avoid unnatural keyword stuffing.
- Maintain luxury editorial tone.
- Be unique for every product.

---

# Content Restrictions

Never mention:

- Cheap
- Affordable
- Best Quality
- Trending
- New Arrival
- Latest Collection
- Must Buy
- Premium Quality
- High Quality
- Elegant Design

Avoid unsupported marketing claims.

Never invent product features not supported by evidence.
