# 1. Purpose

This document serves as the enterprise knowledge base for Product Subtypes within FCOS AI Studio Enterprise.

It provides standardized commercial product subtype taxonomy, construction-based classifications, silhouette-based classifications, hybrid product variants, regional terminology, marketplace mappings, luxury retail terminology, and emerging fashion subtypes used by the FCOS Product Subtype Intelligence Engine.

This document contains product subtype knowledge only and does not define processing rules.
