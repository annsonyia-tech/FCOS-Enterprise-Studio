4. Design Aesthetic × Construction Matrix ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Examples:

Cape → Modern Glamour

Angrakha → Heritage Revival

Layered → Architectural Modernism
5. Controlled Vocabulary Rule ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Maintain a fixed enterprise library of approximately 100–150 approved Design Aesthetic values.

Never create new aesthetic names dynamically.
Normalize supplier terminology to the closest approved aesthetic.
Ensure one consistent Design Aesthetic per product for reliable catalog generation, search, analytics, and recommendation.
