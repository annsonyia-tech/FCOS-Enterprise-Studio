# 10. Fashion Industry Conventions

Long garments generally extend below the knee.

Crop garments terminate above the natural waist.

Floor-length garments lightly touch the floor.

Bridal lehengas generally feature floor-length hemlines.

Anarkalis typically extend from below the knee to floor length.

Sherwanis generally finish between the knee and calf.

---

# 11. Component Relationships

Blouse Length shall be evaluated independently of Lehenga Length.

Kurta Length shall be independent of Pant Length.

Top Length shall not influence Skirt Length.

Each garment component shall maintain its own reference standards.

---

# 12. Visual Measurement Reference

Visual Measurement Engine shall reference:

Women's Wear

5 ft 4 in (163 cm)

Men's Wear

5 ft 10 in (178 cm)

Girls' Wear

4 ft (122 cm)

Boys' Wear

4 ft 2 in (127 cm)

These reference heights support proportional garment-length estimation when manufacturer measurements are unavailable.
