# 13. Body Measurement Definitions

Bust

The fullest circumference of the bust.

Chest

The fullest circumference of the chest.

Waist

Natural waist circumference.

Hip

Fullest hip circumference.

Shoulder

Shoulder point to shoulder point.

Armhole

Circumference around the arm opening.

Sleeve Length

Shoulder seam to sleeve opening.

Inseam

Inside leg measurement.

Outseam

Outside leg measurement.

Rise

Waist to crotch measurement.

# 16. Fashion Industry Sizing Practices

Manufacturer sizing varies by brand.

Body measurements differ from finished garment measurements.

Stitched garments may include alteration margins.

Free-size garments have manufacturer-defined coverage ranges.

Letter sizes and numeric sizes are not universally equivalent and should always be interpreted using manufacturer data when available.
